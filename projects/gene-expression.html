Gene Expression Analysis

A statistical analysis of whether SALL1 expression in subcutaneous adipose tissue is associated with sex.

OverviewResearch questionObjectiveApproachToolsFindingsLimitationsEvidence

Overview

This secondary project used GTEx data for subcutaneous adipose tissue. The analysis included 663 samples: 445 male and 218 female. Sex was coded as 1 = male and 2 = female.

Research question

Is SALL1 expression in subcutaneous adipose tissue associated with sex?

Objective

Compare normalized SALL1 expression between the two sex-coded sample groups and quantify the association using linear regression.

Approach

  1. Used GTEx subcutaneous adipose tissue samples and retained outliers.
  2. Compared mean normalized expression for the male-coded and female-coded groups.
  3. Fit a linear regression and used the Breusch–Pagan test to check for heteroscedasticity.

Tools

  • Python
  • Linear regression
  • Breusch–Pagan test

Findings

Mean normalized SALL1 expression was 0.60 in female samples and −0.32 in male samples, a difference of 0.92. The linear regression found an association (R² = 0.184, p ≈ 4 × 10⁻³¹). The Breusch–Pagan test result was p = 0.975.

These results describe an association in the analyzed samples; they do not establish that sex causes the expression difference.

Limitations

The analysis covers one gene in one tissue. It did not adjust for age or death history. Outliers were retained.
